Assumption of risk

Huginn is an experimental, automated aid for learning to read the weather. It is not a forecast service, it can be wrong, and it does not know what the air at launch is doing right now. Speedflying can kill you. You alone decide whether to fly — check conditions yourself, on site, and follow local site rules and your training.
